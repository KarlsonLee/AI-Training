# Order, Shipping & Returns Compliance Handbook

## 1. Standard Returns
- Customers may return items within **30 days of the delivery date**.
- Items must be unused and in original packaging.
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method within **5-7 business days** after the returned item is inspected.
- Change-of-mind returns are the customer's cost: return shipping is deducted from the refund.

## 2. Damaged or Defective Items
- Damaged or defective items may be returned within **45 days of the delivery date**.
- A **free prepaid return label** is provided. The customer pays nothing for return shipping.
- Photo evidence is required for items reported as "arrived damaged".
- Defective items get a **full refund** or a replacement, at the customer's choice.

## 3. Non-Returnable Cases
- Requests made after the applicable return window are **rejected**.
- Opened software, gift cards and personalised items cannot be returned.

## 4. Refund Approval Limits
- Refunds up to **MYR/USD 500** can be approved by a support agent.
- Refunds **above 500** require **manager approval** before the money is released.
- The analyst never issues refunds itself. It only advises on eligibility.

## 5. Shipping Rules
- Standard delivery takes 3-7 business days.
- Orders in `Processing` status have no tracking number yet.
- If an order is `In Transit` for more than 10 days, open a carrier investigation.

## 6. Inventory Rules
- Stock below the **reorder level** is flagged as "low stock".
- Stock of 0 is "out of stock"; new orders are backordered (14 days).
